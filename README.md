# Streetloop

Streetloop is a working title for an original third-person city sandbox by Chaosmos Studio.
The project begins with one playable harbor block and grows through separately built districts, vehicles, characters, and city systems.

The first milestone is a place to walk, drive, and interact in, with art made for the actual game camera.
The game uses Godot 4.7 and Blender 5.2.

Read [PLAN.md](PLAN.md), [ART_DIRECTION.md](ART_DIRECTION.md), and [ASSET_CATALOG.md](ASSET_CATALOG.md) before adding assets or systems.

## Current playable block

Play the [browser preview](https://chaosmos.studio/streetloop/) on desktop or a landscape phone.
On a phone, use the left stick to move or steer, drag the open upper screen to look, and use the right buttons to run, jump, interact, brake, or exit.

Walk from the harbor sidewalk into the repair shop, use the door and fan switch, read the service manual, and run the fan diagnostic on the workbench terminal.
The Tide hatchback is parked beside the sidewalk. Look at its driver door and press E to enter. Use W and S for throttle and reverse, A and D to steer, Space to brake, and E to exit after stopping.
On foot, use WASD to move, Shift to sprint, Space to jump, the mouse to look, and E to interact. Gamepad movement and interaction are mapped in `project.godot`.
The character creator opens on a new profile. Open it again with C or the Character button, choose an identity and colors, turn the preview, and save to use that character in the harbor block.

The first block and its assets are under active construction.
The [first-drive review](docs/reviews/2026-11-12-first-drive.md) and [service-terminal review](docs/reviews/2026-11-12-service-terminal.md) record measured behavior and visual gaps.
