# Harbor service terminal review

The first service terminal is on the repair shop workbench beside the readable manual.
Its editable Blender source contains 122 objects, including a separate monitor housing, keyboard keys, mouse, stand, and cables.
The Godot glTF has 7 meshes, 7 materials, 10,458 triangles, and a 1,262,076-byte file.
Three housing textures are 512 x 512 pixels, and the authored display texture is 1024 x 640 pixels.

The player opened the terminal through the normal focus interaction, ran a diagnostic with the wall switch off, closed it, switched on the fan, waited for it to spin up, reopened it, passed the diagnostic, and returned to movement.
The diagnostic rejected a stopped fan and passed at 3.776 rad/s.
The service manual route and the enter, drive, brake, and exit car route still passed after the terminal was placed.
The in-world display and the interactive inspection view were checked at 1280 x 720 and 844 x 390.

The case, keyboard, and mouse still need a closer material and ergonomic pass.
The in-world screen is static while the inspection view reads live fan state.
The room lighting and the larger repair shop finish remain first pass.
