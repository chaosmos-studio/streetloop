# Tide hatchback impact check

A 5 m wide, 2.1 m tall static wall was placed across the existing harbor road at z = 22 m for a repeatable contact check.
The car accelerated into it from its parked position at z = 8 m, settled with its center at z = 19.957 m and y = 0.813 m, and measured 0.0003 km/h after 270 physics ticks.
Reverse input then moved the car 9.111 m away in 150 physics ticks.

This verifies one head-on collision and reverse recovery with the current box collider and four suspension contacts.
Oblique impacts, curbs, rollovers, damage, and occupant response remain untested.
