# LIFE

LIFE is the working vision for a playable human life inside a changing city.
Streetloop is the current technical prototype for its movement, vehicles, spaces, and first work activity.

The player begins in a household, grows through childhood and adult life, and can continue through a successor after death.
Daily choices change skills, health, relationships, reputation, money, property, and future opportunities.
Work is played through actions in the world, such as driving a passenger, repairing a vehicle, preparing food, or running a shop.
The player can change jobs, combine careers, start businesses, buy property, pursue crime, or spend time on family and personal interests.
Other residents have homes, routines, goals, finances, and relationships that continue to matter when the player is elsewhere.
The long-term economic path can end in ownership of every eligible private property and major business in the city.
Public places and civic assets retain their public role.

## The day players should feel

The player wakes with a concrete need and a choice about how to spend time.
They travel through an explorable neighborhood, perform work that requires skill, and receive money or another consequence.
They decide how to use that result while household bills, people, property, and new events continue to change.
They return to a home where earlier decisions affect the next day.

## Design commitments

- Every career family starts with one distinctive playable activity and a clear progression path.
- Financial pressure creates choices while absence from the game has a capped, recoverable cost.
- Relationships reflect repeated actions and independent NPC goals.
- Buildings that invite entry have purposeful interiors and usable objects.
- A city district earns expansion through a distinct route, residents, work, and social choices.
- A generation transfer preserves property, debt, relationships, and reputation according to rules the player can inspect.
- Realistic materials, movement, vehicles, and construction are verified inside the game camera.

## Production boundary

The full career list in the original brief is a long-term content map.
The first shippable neighborhood focuses on one household, one school or training path, three active jobs, a small relationship circle, rent and recurring bills, one vehicle, and a few properties.
Later systems reuse these foundations rather than adding dozens of menu-only professions.
