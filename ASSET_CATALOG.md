# Streetloop asset catalog

This is the first 120 individually authored assets, not a generation target.
Each checked row means the asset has its own editable Blender source, Godot-ready export, review views, in-game inspection, and measured cost entry.
Use `art/source/A###_name.blend`, `assets/models/A###_name.glb`, and `art/review/A###_name/`.
Save material sources and textures with the source asset when they are unique.
Store shared material library sources under `art/materials/`.
Keep mechanical subparts separate in one source when they move as one asset.
Mark a row complete only after the actual Godot camera, collision, and contact tests pass.

## Per-asset acceptance record

For every asset, record its intended camera distance, dimensions, facing axis, origin, materials, animation clips, collider, texture sizes, triangle count, draw-call contribution, and known limitations in its review folder.
Keep the checkbox open until Blender views, a Godot import view, and a gameplay view exist.
For animated assets, include a short motion preview and test interruption or loop transitions.
