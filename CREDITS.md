# Asset credits

The A001 anatomical studies and first selectable character identities use the realistic male and female body and eye meshes from [Blender's Human Base Meshes v1.4.1](https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/).
Blender Studio and community contributors released that bundle under CC0.
Streetloop resized and re-unwrapped the meshes, changed their face and body shapes, added materials, clothing, hair, a skeleton, and animation clips, and kept editable Blender sources beside the Godot exports.
The original bundle was downloaded from [Blender's official mirror](https://mirror.blender.org/demo/asset-bundles/human-base-meshes/human-base-meshes-bundle-v1.4.1.zip).
Its SHA-256 is `811f43accbb31a88266d932f8f5563b2d13586fca0ba2693aad1f5fe582b3515`.
