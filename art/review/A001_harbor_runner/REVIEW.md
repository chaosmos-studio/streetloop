# A001 Harbor runner review

The first editable character is 1.77 m high, faces Blender -Y and Godot -Z, and has its origin at the feet.
The source keeps the hip, shoulder, elbow, and knee pivots separate for movement.
The grouped glTF has 29 meshes, 10,748 triangles, 11 materials, five 512 px textures, and a 1,458,596 byte file.
The three Blender views show that the shoulders and hands are still too round, the face is simple, and the workwear needs more convincing folds and weathering.
This is a movement and scale prototype, so the A001 catalog row remains open until animation, gameplay-camera review, contact, and material passes are complete.
