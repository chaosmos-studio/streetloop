# A085 Repair mechanic

The first art pass uses the harbor runner's 1.75 m articulation and adds a short cap, oil-dark apron, tool belt, wrench, pencil, and shop rag.
The editable Blender collection and three unique 512 x 512 cloth textures are saved with the asset.
The exported glTF has 44 nodes, 34 mesh primitives, 17 materials, five embedded images, and 10,160 triangles.
The exported file is 1,460,876 bytes.
Front, side, and rear renders show the cap, apron, and tools from the intended scale.
